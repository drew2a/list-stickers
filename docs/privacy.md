# Privacy policy for List Stickers

Last updated: 22 November 2026

List Stickers is a browser extension that lets you put private notes on items
of lists and on pages of websites.

## What the extension stores

To show your notes again, the extension keeps, in your browser's local
extension storage on your own device:

- the text and status mark of each note you write;
- the address and title of the page or item the note belongs to;
- where you dragged each sticker;
- for each website, which elements of its pages you marked as a list.

## What leaves your device

Nothing. The extension makes no network requests. It has no server, no
account, no sync, no analytics and no advertising. Its author never receives
your notes or any information about the pages you visit.

## What the extension reads

The extension runs on the pages you open so that it can draw your stickers
there. It reads the structure and link addresses of a page only to find the
items of a list you pointed at and to recognise them on your next visit. It
does not read what you type into forms, passwords or payment details.

## Sharing

No data is collected, so none is sold, shared or transferred to anyone.

## Keeping and deleting your notes

Your notes stay until you delete them. You can delete a single note in its
sticker, or any note on the extension's "All notes" page. Removing the
extension from the browser deletes everything it stored. The "All notes" page
can save your notes to a file on your computer; that file is yours and the
extension does not send it anywhere.
